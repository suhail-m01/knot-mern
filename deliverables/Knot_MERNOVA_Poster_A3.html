01 Abstract

A cross-platform MERN + Flutter ecosystem engineered for hyperlocal tech discovery across Bengaluru neighbourhoods. Built upon a shared Express 5 REST API, Knot integrates an interactive Leaflet 10 km spatial explorer, Socket.IO real-time seat tracking, and portable digital QR ticket passes.

The platform eliminates ticket overbooking with atomic ACID seat claims, ingests third-party hackathon listings safely, and empowers organizers with instant mobile camera check-ins.

02 Problem Statement

Tech events in metropolitan hubs like Bengaluru are fragmented across WhatsApp groups, disparate portals, and social channels. Prospective participants encounter:

  • Stale & Oversold Listings: Seat counts lack single sources of truth, causing attendees to arrive at exhausted venues.
  • Unknown Commute Feasibility: Listings hide exact coordinates, preventing commuters from gauging travel radius.
  • Chaotic Venue Gate Control: Organizers rely on paper lists or slow spreadsheet check-ins prone to fraud.
03 Objectives
  • Hyperlocal Radius Filtering: Anchor discovery to Bengaluru tech hubs (Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR) within a default 10 km perimeter.
  • Zero Overbooking Concurrency: Execute conditional atomic DB seat allocations with automated FIFO waitlist transitions.
  • Unified MERN + Flutter Synchronization: Connect Web and Mobile clients to one backend with shared JWT sessions.
  • High-Definition Gate Validation: Generate cryptographic QR passes and camera-based sub-second doorstep validation.
  • Ethical Hackathon Ingestion: Harvesters parse public APIs (Devfolio, Unstop) as reviewable drafts respecting robots.txt.

04 Proposed Solution

A dual-client, single-backend architecture delivering an uncompromising source of truth: 10 km Geospatial Discovery → ACID Conditional Seat Locking → Socket.IO Live Broadcast → Encrypted QR Validation.

Seats cannot be oversold due to database-level transactional locks. Attendees hold immutable digital QR passes, while organizers gain real-time capacity analytics, AI concierge matchmaking, and draft review controls.
